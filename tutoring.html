Every student starts from a different place. Before we focus on assignments or exam preparation, I work to identify the underlying concepts that need strengthening. By addressing those foundations first, students build understanding that lasts beyond a single test or course.

I've been teaching and tutoring mathematics since 2015 — as a Teaching Assistant at UC Santa Cruz, a tutor at UC Berkeley, and a private tutor throughout that time. Working one-on-one with students has always been one of the most rewarding parts of my career, and I enjoy adapting my teaching approach to each student's background, goals, and learning style.

If you're interested in working together, I'd be happy to discuss your goals and find a time that works.
