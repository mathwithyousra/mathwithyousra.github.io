Hi, I'm Yousra.

Mathematician & Educator

I've spent over a decade making math make sense.

I hold a B.A. in Mathematics from UC Berkeley and an M.A. in Mathematics from UC Santa Cruz. I also worked as an AI/Machine Learning Scientist for the Navy, applying rigorous mathematical reasoning to real research problems.

But my real track record is in the classroom. Since 2015, I've taught and tutored Algebra, Geometry, AP Calculus, Linear Algebra, Differential Equations, and Discrete Mathematics — as a Teaching Assistant at UC Santa Cruz, a tutor at UC Berkeley, and a private tutor the entire time in between. I learned not only how to teach and tutor, but how to tailor my teaching style to each student's needs and background. Math is something I am deeply passionate about — it's part of who I am, and something I want to share with my students so it stays with them in their careers.

I don't teach shortcuts or tricks to get fast at math. I work with each student individually to understand their learning gaps and teach the why, so it holds up under exam pressure and stays with you after the class ends.

I offer 1:1 tutoring over Zoom for high school and undergraduate students, SAT/ACT math prep over Zoom, and self-paced online courses — with video lessons, practice exams, and worksheets.
