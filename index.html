Hi, I'm Yousra.

Mathematician & Educator

I'm a mathematician and teacher with a B.A. in Mathematics from UC Berkeley and an M.A. in Mathematics from UC Santa Cruz. Over the past several years I've taught calculus, linear algebra, and differential equations as a Teaching Assistant at UCSC, and tutored students one-on-one from Algebra 1 through advanced university coursework.

Before returning to pure mathematics, I worked as an AI/Machine Learning Scientist at the Naval Information Warfare Center Pacific, applying rigorous mathematical reasoning to real research problems.

I built Math With Yousra to bring that same rigor to students directly — university-aligned, proof-based instruction with no shortcuts, whether you're catching up, keeping up, or getting ahead.
