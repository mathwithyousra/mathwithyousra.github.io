Hi, I'm Yousra.

Mathematician & Educator

I've spent over a decade making math make sense.

I hold a B.A. in Mathematics from UC Berkeley and an M.A. in Mathematics from UC Santa Cruz. I also worked as an AI/Machine Learning Scientist at NIWC Pacific, applying advanced mathematics to predictive machine learning models and high-dimensional data systems.

Alongside my research experience, teaching and tutoring mathematics have been a central part of my career. Since 2015, I've taught and tutored Algebra, Geometry, Trigonometry, Calculus, Linear Algebra, Differential Equations, and Discrete Mathematics — as a Teaching Assistant at UC Santa Cruz, a tutor at UC Berkeley, and a private tutor throughout that time. Along the way, I've learned not only how to explain mathematics clearly, but how to adapt my teaching to each student's background, goals, and learning style. Math is something I am deeply passionate about — it's part of who I am, and I hope my students leave with a deeper understanding and appreciation for it long after the course is over.

Math With Yousra is a university-aligned online mathematics academy designed to help students build lasting mathematical understanding at their own pace. Each course is structured around rigorous university syllabi and includes comprehensive video lessons, original worksheets, practice exams, and step-by-step solution guides. The goal is not simply to teach students how to solve problems, but to teach them the mathematical reasoning behind the solutions so their understanding holds up under exam pressure and extends beyond a single semester.

For students seeking additional guidance, I also offer a limited number of 1:1 tutoring sessions each semester. Learn more on the tutoring page.
